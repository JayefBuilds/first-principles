# Checkout confirmation incident

## Scope

On 2026-08-14, checkout confirmation failed for customers routed through the EU West region. The incident lasted 31 minutes. Payment authorization continued, but successful orders did not receive a confirmation page or email until processing recovered.

## Customer impact

Support received 47 contacts during the incident window. No duplicate charges were found in the payment provider report. Some customers retried checkout because the confirmation page did not appear.

## Status

The incident is resolved. Follow up work is tracked separately and is outside this reading exercise.
